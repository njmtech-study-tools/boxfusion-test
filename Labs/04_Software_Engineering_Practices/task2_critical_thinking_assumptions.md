## ❓ Problem Statement

**Passage**: A survey revealed that companies involving customers in their early product development stages have a 16% higher customer satisfaction rate post-launch. So, incorporating customer feedback during product development might enhance user adoption for a new product.

Which one of the following is an assumption upon which the passage's argument depends?

---

## 🔘 Options
1. The survey interviewed companies creating similar products to each other.
2. Costs to involve customers in early product development are offset by increased revenue from higher customer satisfaction.
3. Increased customer satisfaction results in increased user adoption.
4. A 16% customer satisfaction rate boost will impact a company's profitability.

---

## ✅ Correct Answer
**Option 3: Increased customer satisfaction results in increased user adoption.**

---

## 🔍 Detailed Explanation

1. **Premise vs Conclusion**:
   - **Premise**: Early customer involvement $\rightarrow$ +16% customer satisfaction.
   - **Conclusion**: Early customer feedback $\rightarrow$ enhanced user adoption.

2. **The Unstated Gap**:
   - The premise speaks about *customer satisfaction*, while the conclusion claims *user adoption*.
   - To logically bridge satisfaction to adoption, the argument inherently assumes that **higher customer satisfaction leads to higher user adoption**.
