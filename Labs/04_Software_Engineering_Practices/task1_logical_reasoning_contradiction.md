## ❓ Problem Statement

Consider the following two statements:

- **Statement 1**: Being efficient at work means balancing the time and effort it takes to accomplish a task with the value of the completed task. A task that takes a lot of time and energy should have a high completion value or else it's not efficient to complete. Being efficient requires wisdom in addition to other skills.
- **Statement 2**: The most efficient employees are the ones who don't question what tasks they are assigned and just say "yes" to whatever they are asked.

Which of the following best describes the logical relationship between these statements?

---

## 🔘 Options
1. Statement 2 contradicts Statement 1
2. Statement 2 supports Statement 1
3. Statement 2 restates Statement 1
4. Statement 2 is irrelevant to Statement 1

---

## ✅ Correct Answer
**Option 1: Statement 2 contradicts Statement 1**

---

## 🔍 Detailed Explanation

1. **Statement 1 Assertions**:
   - Work efficiency requires evaluating task value against effort.
   - It explicitly requires **wisdom** to filter out low-value, high-effort tasks.

2. **Statement 2 Assertions**:
   - Asserts that efficient employees blindly accept all tasks without questioning ("just say 'yes' to whatever they are asked").

3. **Logical Conflict**:
   - Blindly accepting all tasks without questioning directly precludes applying wisdom or evaluating task value vs effort. Therefore, Statement 2 contradicts Statement 1.
